Mobile combat controls

              

                Drag controls in the preview to reposition them. Portrait and landscape layouts save

                Drag a control, then release to save. Arrow keys also move a focused control.

Keyboard & controller Bindings & reference

              

              

                Gamepad: left stick move, right stick camera, RB attack, RT heavy, LB guard, B roll,
                X heal, Y ability, R3 or LT lock, L3 sprint, A hold-revive, D-pad up ping, Start
                pause. In menus, D-pad or left stick navigates, A selects, and B returns. Left/right
                adjusts a focused slider.
              

              
Mouse & keyboard bindings

              

                Default controls: WASD move, Q ability, E heavy attack, R heal, F revive, Space
                roll, Shift sprint. Left mouse attacks, right mouse guards, middle-click locks on,
                and middle-drag orbits the camera. Mouse button 4 pings; the on-screen Ping button
                also works. Select a binding, then press a key or mouse button. Escape cancels; P
                and Escape always pause. Mouse and touch controls stay available.

      THE ART OF SURVIVAL
      
Steel. Stamina. Timing.

      

        This is a third-person action arena. Move freely, face your enemy, and fight at sword range.
        There are no turns or tile-based controls. The guide’s prose uses default keys; the bindings
        below reflect your saved settings.
      

      

        The Bestiary tab records enemies encountered in solo or co-op and explains their moves and
        counters. Combat warning cues and directional audio can be toggled in Settings. Mobile
        controls have separate portrait/landscape layouts: drag buttons in the Settings preview,
        adjust size and opacity, or mirror the layout. Touch attack lock-on assist and a mobile
        performance mode are optional. Revive and ritual interaction share the HOLD USE touch
        button.
      

      

        Select your chess piece in the armory. Press Q for its special ability: pawn diagonal
        capture, knight L-vault, bishop diagonals, rook file charge, queen eight-way strike, or king
        Castling Ward. Each costs 35 stamina and has a cooldown. Enemy special attacks have a
        longer, marked windup.

        Watch for orange windups, then dodge the impact or face the attack and
        guard. Bishops cast projectiles, knights leap, rooks charge and slam, and the queen
        unleashes a wide sweep. Attacking and guarding consume stamina. It regenerates when you
        rest.
      

      

        The Ashen Board ends with the Iron Castellan. Leave its marked charge lane, punish the
        exposed core, and break its posture for a longer opening. Phase II adds double hammer slams
        and cross-board attacks. The boss challenge lets you face it immediately without granting a
        campaign clear. Clear five campaign encounters to earn promotion. Cleared encounters restore
        vitality according to the selected difficulty.
      

      

        Choose a longsword, rapier, or warhammer in the armory. Press E or use Heavy for a
        weapon-specific strike: longsword sweep, retreating rapier thrust, or a charged hammer slam.
        Heavy attacks cost more stamina, bypass enemy frontal guards, and leave a longer recovery. A
        fresh guard within 0.18 seconds of impact performs a perfect parry and empowers your next
        attack for 1.8 seconds. Holding guard remains a normal block. Release guard for at least
        0.25 seconds before attempting another parry. Between encounters, choose one blessing; co-op
        waits for both players. Versus uses unmodified weapons.
      

      

        Guarded enemies take reduced frontal damage: flank them, finish a three-hit combo, use a
        warhammer, or earn a riposte. Gold-marked elites are tougher. Standard begins with a guarded
        pawn, a flanker and a knight. Enemies close in faster while you drink a flask; create
        distance before healing. Holding guard slows stamina recovery, and Standard restores only 10
        vitality between encounters; Pilgrim starts with three pawns. Realm-colored floor markings
        warn of arena strikes; leave before they turn red. The garden targets a root circle, the
        cathedral uses narrow frost lanes, and the eclipse court strikes diagonally. Queens chain
        three attacks, so wait for the full recovery. In co-op, hold your Revive binding near an
        ally and use Ping to mark your position.
      

      

        Solo checkpoints save the start of each encounter, including your build, health, flasks,
        position and ability cooldown. Continue from the campaign menu or retry after death.
        Full-run challenge restarts at encounter one. Boss relics unlock in Armory. In co-op, break
        posture and have your partner land a heavy attack during the opening for a team finisher
        with +40 base damage.
      

      

        The Eclipse Court ends with the Fallen King. His royal edicts command and replace a limited
        number of guards. At half vitality, he sacrifices surviving guards and gains new attacks
        before fighting alone. Leave the marked cross lanes, dodge both sword sweeps, then punish
        his exposed crown. Co-op expeditions now have six encounters, ending with the Castellan,
        Confessor and Fallen King.
      

      

        Ser Veyr retreats from the first cavalry encounter and returns scarred in solo encounter IV.
        He adapts to light attacks, heavy attacks or frequent guarding. His red second-leap tell
        breaks guard: dodge it. In co-op he returns alongside the Castellan. Between fights, bargain
        for supplies, challenge an extra wounded knight for souls, or spend souls at the forge.
        Continue is always free.
      

      

        Spear thrusts have long reach and a narrow point. Twin blades trade reach and guard stamina
        for fast wide cuts. The two-handed greatsword has sweeping light attacks and a committed
        charged heavy. In co-op, fire a rook and bishop ability within 1.5 seconds so their lanes
        cross for a 50-damage crossing explosion. A king ability paired with an ally ability within
        five units and 1.5 seconds wards both allies for four seconds. Ordinary ability stamina and
        cooldowns still apply.
      

      

        Pawns earn one promotion after surviving encounter II. At the next intermission, become a
        knight, bishop, rook or queen, or hold your pawn rank. Your equipped weapon and blessings
        stay; the new piece ability is immediately ready. Other starting pieces keep their chosen
        class. Co-op pauses until both players finish their choices.
      

      

        Arena goals appear in regular encounters: hold an uncontested gold throne ring for eight
        seconds, break three red ritual sigils by holding your Revive / Interact binding nearby for
        two seconds each, or protect the wounded king for eighteen seconds. Moving, guarding or
        taking damage interrupts a ritual channel; reviving a nearby ally takes priority. The king
        can be attacked by enemies; if he falls, the encounter is lost. Finish the arena goal and
        defeat the enemies to advance and claim 150 bonus souls per player. Killing all attackers
        also secures a surviving king. Boss fights and sparring keep their own rules.
      

      

        Each intermission asks for at most one choice: a passage, promotion, camp event or blessing.
        Extra blessings are granted automatically. On your first passage, choose to recover at the
        sanctuary, face a champion for 200 souls and an extra blessing at the next intermission, or
        fight two additional knights for 150 souls and a flask. Clear the whole encounter to earn
        its reward. Co-op requires matching route votes; either player can change their vote.
        Champions have distinct counters: break the Iron Marshal’s guard to strip nearby allies’
        wards, dodge both Red Huntmaster leaps, or evade both Ashen Cantor volleys before punishing
        their longer recovery.
      

      

        The Pale Cathedral ends with the Pale Confessor. Its doubles cast no shadows. Dodge crossed
        bolts and punish the recovery; phase II fires a delayed second volley. Co-op expeditions
        face the Castellan, Confessor, then the Fallen King.
      

      

        Pause a solo run and choose Save & Quit to keep the exact fight on this device. Optional
        curses and co-op roles are in the armory. Boss Rush faces three bosses with only two flasks
        and no free healing. Complete a combat bonus to reveal a secret gold arch; hold Interact
        there to summon an optional Regent. Amber rings warn you for two seconds before arena gates
        rise or a pillar falls. On phones, drag the open arena to orbit, pinch to zoom, and use
        Camera ↺ to reset.
